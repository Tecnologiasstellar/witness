# Javan Rhino (Rhinoceros sondaicus) — Fact Pack
Compiled 2026-08-26 (research agent, web-verified). For chapter drafting; every claim sourced; conflicts reported side by side, never averaged.

## 1. Current status
- THE COUNT DISPUTE IS THE STORY: IUCN Asian/African Rhino Specialist Groups (Aug 13, 2025): ~50, down from 76 in 2022 after poaching (mongabay.com/2025/08/javan-rhino-numbers-plunge...). IRF State of the Rhino 2025: same ~50 (rhinos.org/blog/news-room/global-rhino-population-estimates-2025) while IRF species page notes camera traps show ~75. Indonesia's Ministry (KLHK, World Rhino Day Sept 2025): 87–100, Spatial Count Model (en.antaranews.com/news/381452). ANTARA Feb 2026: "fewer than 80". Save the Rhino: ~50 (savetherhino.org). REPORT BOTH — never pick one.
- Poaching scandal: two gangs (leaders Sunendi and Sahru/Suhar) operated 2019–2023; exposed May 29, 2023 via missing camera traps; 13 arrested; confessions cover 18–26 rhinos killed (mongabay 2024/05; cbsnews). Convictions: Sunendi 12 years June 2024 — record wildlife-crime sentence in Indonesia, personally killed 6 (mongabay 2024/06); Feb 12, 2025 five more at 11 years, second leader 12 years (rhinos.org/blog/more-justice-for-javan-rhinos); horn trader Yogi Purwadi 4.5 years; broker Liem Hoo Kwan Willy convicted by Supreme Court May 2025, 1 year (mongabay 2025/05).

## 2. The past (threat chain)
- Once ranged Bangladesh → SE Asia → Sumatra/Java (traffic.org). Described by Desmarest, 1822.
- Horn hunting (TCM + status ornaments) + lowland forest conversion collapsed the range. Colonial-era kill counts UNVERIFIED — keep qualitative.
- Vietnam extinction: relict pop rediscovered 1988 in Cat Tien; last individual found dead April 2010, bullet in leg, horn removed; dung genetics showed one animal; declared extinct Oct 25, 2011 (traffic.org/news/javan-rhino-extinct-in-viet-nam).
- Ujung Kulon monitoring began 1967 at ~25; climbed to est. 76 by 2022 (a-z-animals count history article). Krakatoa-1883-depopulation framing is widely repeated but UNVERIFIED to primary source.

## 3. The present
- Single-site risk documented: entire species in one park. Dec 22, 2018 Anak Krakatau collapse tsunami: 400+ human deaths, >10 m run-ups near park, two park officers killed; rhinos happened to be on sheltered southern coast (mongabay 2018/12; nhess.copernicus.org/articles/20/933/2020).
- Camera traps are the only way anyone sees one; Auriga Nusantara 2023: 18 rhinos unseen since 2019 (foreshadowed the scandal).
- Arenga (langkap) palm: invasive, ~18,000 ha ≈ 60% of peninsula, shades out ~250 food species; removal since 2010 (mongabay 2014/07).
- Births continue: calf filmed March 4, 2024; calf recorded Jan 29, 2026, 10:17 p.m., with known mother ARUM — first recorded birth of 2026 (en.antaranews.com/news/402946).

## 4. The future (levers)
- Second site: Cikepuh WR long favored but overlaps military training ground; search put on hold 2019 (mongabay 2019/07).
- Instead: within-park translocation to fenced JRSCA (~5,100 ha). FIRST-EVER translocation Nov 2025: male MUSOFA captured Nov 3, moved Nov 5, died Nov 7; necropsy (IPB Univ): chronic severe parasitic infection; translocation-stress question open; experts say continue anyway (mongabay 2025/12).
- Genetics: low diversity, ~2:1 male-skewed sex ratio worsened by poachers targeting males; two genetic lines only (IRF; mongabay 2025/12).
- Other: arenga clearance (park at/near capacity per IRF), hardened anti-poaching (gangs used stolen camera-trap data to hunt), sustained prosecutions under 2024 Conservation Act.

## 5. Timeline
1822 described (Desmarest) · 1907 last captive dies, Adelaide Zoo — none in captivity since (savetherhino.org) · 1967 Ujung Kulon monitoring begins ~25 · Apr 2010 last Vietnamese rhino shot · Oct 25, 2011 Vietnam subpopulation declared extinct · Dec 22, 2018 tsunami hits park coast · 2019–2023 gangs kill up to 26; May 2023 exposure; 13 arrests · Jun 2024–May 2025 convictions (12 yr record etc.) · Nov 2025 first translocation ends in Musofa's death · Jan 29, 2026 Arum's calf recorded.

## 6. Known / Unknown
KNOWN: all wild Javan rhinos in Ujung Kulon; zero in captivity since 1907 (no backup population — extinction there is extinction, full stop); poaching gangs/arrests/sentences; 2018 tsunami proved single-site risk; births in 2023, 2024, 2026.
UNKNOWN: true count (50 vs 87–100 vs <80 — methods disagree by 2×); exact poaching toll (courts confirmed subsets; 18–26 honest range); volcano/tsunami odds (no credible per-year probability); whether translocation stress contributed to Musofa's death.

## 7. Character details
- Single horn usually <20 cm; many females hornless (smallest horn of any rhino — nearly annihilated for the least horn).
- Day-long mud wallows (thermoregulation, skin, parasites). Solitary. Dense-rainforest browser — effectively never seen by humans; known via timestamped night camera-trap footage.
- No Javan rhino in any zoo anywhere. Named individuals: Arum (mother 2026), Musofa (translocated male, died Nov 2025).
