# Content, trust, and rights policy

Last updated: 2026-08-20

## Purpose

Witness cannot ask users to care while being careless with facts, cultural context, sensitive locations, or creators' rights. Content is product infrastructure and must pass the same kind of release gate as code.

## Source hierarchy

Use the strongest available sources for each claim:

1. Species assessment or recovery-plan material from an authorized primary conservation authority.
2. Peer-reviewed research or official government/university/museum material.
3. Established conservation organizations with relevant field expertise.
4. Reputable secondary references for orientation only.

Do not treat Wikipedia as the sole factual source. It can help discover primary sources and rights-cleared media.

## Important IUCN constraint

The IUCN Red List API states that commercial use through the API is forbidden without an appropriate arrangement and may restrict mobile-app use. Witness contains a purchase and must therefore not copy, cache, or build on Red List API data unless written permission or a suitable commercial license is obtained.

The app may use independently sourced facts and careful status wording, but any IUCN trademark, label, assessment text, API data, or derived dataset use must be reviewed separately. A citation alone is not permission.

Official source: https://api.iucnredlist.org/

## Media policy

Every production asset needs a rights record. Acceptable starting points:

- original commissioned/created artwork with a written rights record;
- public-domain media whose status is verified;
- CC0 or CC BY media with complete per-file attribution;
- CC BY-SA only after confirming the derivative and distribution obligations fit the app and share-card use.

Reject or hold:

- unknown-license or social-media images;
- editorial-only assets;
- noncommercial licenses for the commercial app;
- assets whose author, source page, or license version cannot be traced;
- AI visuals that imitate a living artist or are presented as documentary photographs;
- generated depictions that fail a basic species-accuracy review.

Wikimedia Commons is a discovery source, not a blanket license. Verify each file and preserve author, source, license, attribution, and derivative obligations.

Official reuse guidance: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:REUSE

## Sensitive species

- Never publish nest, den, roost, spawning, or precise occurrence coordinates.
- Generalize the range to a level safe for public education.
- Treat data redaction/generalization from source publishers as intentional.
- Have an expert or authoritative policy review any location that could enable collection, harassment, poaching, or disturbance.

## Editorial voice

- Poetic but factual.
- Concrete, spare, and respectful.
- No invented first-person voice for an animal.
- No human blame without evidence and context.
- No certainty where the science is uncertain.
- No “last one” claims unless the scope and date are precise.
- Distinguish extinct, extinct in the wild, possibly extinct, and threatened.
- Give conservation actors and Indigenous/local knowledge appropriate context and attribution.

## Action policy

An action is publishable only if it is:

- relevant to the featured species or its threat system;
- achievable by the intended user in the stated geography;
- linked to a credible destination;
- honest about effort and expected effect;
- reviewed and dated;
- not a disguised ad or unverified fundraising claim.

Action measurement must name its evidence level:

- `opened`: the destination was opened;
- `self_reported`: the user says they completed it;
- `partner_verified`: an external partner supplied outcome evidence.

Only the third category can support a partner-verified outcome claim.

## Content acceptance gate

A species cannot enter the production catalog until:

- scientific and common names are checked;
- status wording and date are checked;
- every factual sentence maps to a source;
- the action and destination work;
- media rights and attribution are complete;
- sensitive-location review passes;
- copy edit and tone review pass;
- the last-verified date is recorded;
- the app rendering is visually and accessibly reviewed.
