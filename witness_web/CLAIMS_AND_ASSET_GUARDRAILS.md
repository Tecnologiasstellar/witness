# Claims and asset guardrails

> Historical v1 snapshot. It contains obsolete catalog and media quantities. `PUBLIC_CLAIMS_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` is the sole current website claim ledger; repository release gates remain higher authority.

## Claim taxonomy

| Safe to claim now | Do not claim now |
|---|---|
| Native SwiftUI iOS MVP | App Store availability or a public download |
| Offline-first bundled local ritual | A hosted service, live collective count, or synced global archive |
| One bundled Vaquita prototype record | A production-ready launch catalog |
| Private on-device Witness and reflection | Accounts, public memories, cross-device sync, or moderation |
| Sources are part of the product model | Every future content card is approved or partner reviewed |
| Daily core intended to remain free | A working subscription, purchase, RevenueCat integration, or donation program |
| A Witness measures attention | That a Witness saves, protects, funds, or changes an outcome |
| Current action opens NOAA information | That users completed an action or achieved an ecological effect |

## Content rules

- Keep the Vaquita status exactly as the reviewed current bundled record states: `Endangered`. Do not silently replace it with another label, even if a different external source later uses one.
- Do not publish a current population number. The app intentionally omits it because it is volatile and not part of the reviewed prototype record.
- Do not turn source-backed threat context into blame, urgency rhetoric, or a claim that any individual action solves it.
- Preserve the distinction among prototype, pending review, approved, unavailable, and error states.

## Asset rules

- DailyArt/Mobbin screenshots are research-only and excluded from the repository. Never use, trace, crop, upload, or reference them in the website output.
- The available Vaquita visual is a locally drawn abstract prototype. Label it as such.
- The user-provided Vaquita image is not available in the workspace and is pending file-level rights metadata. Do not use it.
- Do not use a third-party photo, illustration, map, icon, or texture without a recorded web-compatible right/license/attribution decision.
- Do not include exact species locations, nesting sites, GPS data, or detailed maps.

## Privacy and legal rules

- Do not collect email or analytics in v1.0 without a documented purpose, vendor, consent/disclosure, retention decision, and privacy-policy update.
- Do not imply a nonprofit relationship, partner review, donation flow, revenue donation, or charitable outcome without written, auditable proof.
- If an external source is linked, link directly to the verified source page and identify the source organization.

## Release-language rule

Until every relevant release gate has dated production evidence, use `in development`, `local prototype`, or `iOS MVP`—never `launched`, `available now`, `production-ready`, or `live`.
