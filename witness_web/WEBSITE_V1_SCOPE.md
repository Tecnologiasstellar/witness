# Website v1.0 scope and content plan

> Historical design brief. The implemented multi-route site and its current claims are documented in `README.md`, `site/README.md`, and `PUBLIC_CLAIMS_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`. Do not use the old quantities or availability statements below as current evidence.

## Goal

Create one exceptionally clear page that introduces Witness, shows the ritual, demonstrates its ethical product principles, and offers a credible project link—without pretending the iOS MVP is a public product already.

## Primary conversion

The primary action is: **Explore the ritual** — an on-page anchor to the three-step loop and the Vaquita specimen note.

The secondary action is: **View the project** — an external link to `https://github.com/Tecnologiasstellar/witness`.

Do not add signup, download, “join the movement,” donation, waitlist, payment, or App Store CTAs in v1.0.

## Required one-page sequence

### 1. Header

- Wordmark: `Witness`.
- Anchors: `The ritual`, `Built with care`.
- External text link: `View project ↗`.
- No oversized navigation or marketing badges.

### 2. Hero — the invitation

**Eyebrow:** `A DAILY PRACTICE OF ATTENTION`

**H1:** `Meet one species. Remember what is still here.`

**Support copy:** `A quiet iPhone ritual for seeing, understanding, and taking one honest action.`

**Primary CTA:** `Explore the ritual`

**Secondary CTA:** `View project ↗`

Hero figure: an original abstract Vaquita specimen plate on paper, with the labels `VAQUITA`, `Phocoena sinus`, and `ABSTRACT PROTOTYPE DEPICTION`. Do not show a count, status estimate, or photo.

### 3. The proposition — one species is sufficient

Use a full-width editorial composition, not a card grid.

**Headline:** `The biodiversity crisis is vast. Attention can begin with one name.`

Copy should say that Witness gives one species enough space to be known before asking for action. Avoid implying that attention alone changes conservation outcomes.

### 4. The ritual — three deliberate steps

Use an asymmetric three-part composition with numbered plate marks:

1. **Meet** — encounter one species and a short sourced story.
2. **Witness** — record a private act of attention, once.
3. **Act** — open one credible, source-backed next step.

Add a short clarifier below: `A Witness is a record of attention—not a claim of conservation impact.`

### 5. A field note — the Vaquita

Present a restrained specimen note with an original abstract line figure.

Required facts only:

- `Vaquita · Phocoena sinus`
- `Northern Gulf of California, Mexico`
- `The world’s smallest porpoise lives nowhere else.`
- `Gillnet entanglement is the threat described in Witness’s current bundled story.`

Include “Read the source at NOAA Fisheries ↗” only if the final implementation links directly to the current verified NOAA URL in the catalog. Include an explicit prototype label and a short source/verification note.

### 6. Built with care

Use a horizontal rule-led list, not feature cards:

- **Evidence visible** — sources and verification state belong in the experience.
- **Private by default** — no account or public memories in v1.
- **Offline-first** — the core ritual stays available without a network.
- **Free at the core** — the daily story, Witness action, sources, and credible action stay free.

### 7. Current status

Plain, unambiguous status block:

> `Witness is an iOS MVP in development. It is not yet available on the App Store.`

Supporting text may state that the local prototype includes one bundled species, private on-device Witness persistence, reflections, and an original share preview. It must not claim hosted collective counts, production partners, or production-ready media.

CTA: `View the project ↗`

### 8. Footer

- `Witness` wordmark and one-sentence pitch.
- GitHub link.
- A compact truth note: `Sources, rights, and release readiness are treated as product requirements.`
- No fabricated copyright year if the site is generated from a static template; use the current year dynamically or omit it.

## Tone rules

Use: quiet, precise, inviting, reverent, source-aware, and concrete.

Avoid: “save the planet,” “join the movement,” “last chance,” “vanishing forever,” “every tap helps,” “make an impact today,” “our community,” “powered by AI,” “revolutionary,” and all fabricated metrics/testimonials.

## Acceptance criteria

- A visitor understands the product and its ethical boundary in under 30 seconds.
- The page clearly distinguishes the working prototype from a public App Store release.
- Every present-tense product claim is supported by the repository state.
- No unverified media, unlicensed reference material, sensitive coordinate, live count, impact outcome, or nonexistent partner appears.
- The page is responsive from 320 px through large desktop, keyboard navigable, and WCAG AA for text/controls.
- The primary CTA is visible without scrolling on a typical mobile viewport.
- The visual system feels recognizably Witness/Atlas, not DailyArt or a generic eco SaaS page.
